# PRODUCTION RULES — NON-NEGOTIABLE

## NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTENT QUALITY STANDARD — APPLY TO EVERY DELIVERABLE

This section preserves the user's production instructions and is a required QA gate. It applies to all six brands, all 60 deliverables, every revision, every export, and every future agent handoff.

### 1. Every asset must have a clear job

- Every post, carousel, story, Reel/video, ad, and campaign asset must explicitly serve **TOFU, MOFU, or BOFU**. The funnel stage must be appropriate to the audience's relationship with the brand and the asset's actual objective.
- Label every deliverable with its real placement and format, not a vague internal title. Examples: `TOFU — Brand discovery — Instagram feed carousel`; `MOFU — Ingredient education — Instagram Story sequence`; `BOFU — New product campaign — paid social ad`; `BOFU — In-store conversion — Sintra-board counter signage`; `MOFU — Product consideration — online-store listing cover`; `BOFU — Menu selection — table menu`.
- If a graphic does not clearly serve a funnel objective, do not force it into the content plan. It must instead be a useful, brand-appropriate operational or commercial asset: Sintra-board signage, packaging design/mockup, table menu, menu insert, online-store listing cover, e-commerce category tile, product information card, retail display, loyalty card, event collateral, or another real use case for that brand and its channels/products.
- Do not make graphics merely because they look attractive. Every element, illustration, icon, pattern, headline, image and layout must have a reason to exist and support the message, product, platform, or use case.

### 2. No generic filler and no visible placeholder copy

- Do not put visible filler such as `Ingredients`, `Price`, `Product Name`, `Lorem ipsum`, `Placeholder`, `[insert copy]`, or empty generic labels into finished designs.
- When a real-world detail is not yet supplied, use a coherent, realistic **working example** (for example, an actual dish name with plausible ingredients, a considered product name, or a proposed sample price) and keep it editable in Figma so the user can revise it during post-production. Do not visually label it as a placeholder.
- Clearly document assumed sample details in the handoff or production notes as `working sample — verify before publication`; do not present them as confirmed business facts.
- Never invent testimonials, customer reviews, endorsements, certifications, clinical/health outcomes, product performance claims, legal claims, stock availability, delivery promises, dates, contact details, QR destinations, or factual business credentials. Use a truthful alternative or leave the claim out until evidence is supplied.
- For skincare, fragrance, food and other regulated/safety-sensitive categories, keep claims conservative and substantiated. A realistic working ingredient or dish description is acceptable as editable creative copy; an unsupported efficacy, allergen, nutrition, safety, or medical claim is not.

### 3. Brand distinction is mandatory

- The brand books are the official identity source. Use their exact approved palette, typography, logo/icon system, graphic language, image treatment, tone of voice, and target audience. Do not revert to older styles or blend brands into one shared aesthetic.
- Distinction must be visible at a glance through color behavior, typography, composition, graphic devices, image direction, and content voice—not just through swapping a logo or accent color.
- Avoid making every brand look like the same premium editorial/serif template. Follow the brand-specific design system and choose a visual treatment because it belongs to that brand and communicates that asset's purpose.
- Keep vector logos, icons, patterns and editable graphic masters as native Figma elements where possible. AI imagery is supporting visual material; generated logo artwork is not a replacement for the editable master.

### 4. Platform and production logic

- Select formats based on where and how the asset will actually be used: feed post, carousel, Story, Reel, paid ad, e-commerce listing, online-store banner, packaging, menu, table insert, physical signage, retail display, or other appropriate channel/collateral.
- Make each item in a carousel or story sequence progress the message; do not pad sequences with repetitive slides.
- Respect platform-specific safe areas, dimensions, text hierarchy, readability, CTA placement, sticker zones, and export requirements.
- For generated mockups, keep logos, wordmarks, product labels and exact copy out of the generated image whenever they can be added as editable Figma layers. Preserve clean surfaces for editable branding.
- Use actual supplied product/brand references faithfully. Do not invent a product SKU, packaging structure, product feature, or physical application that conflicts with the brand's real offering.

### 5. Required pre-delivery QA checklist

Before marking any asset complete, verify all of the following:

- [ ] It has a named TOFU/MOFU/BOFU role or a documented operational/collateral purpose.
- [ ] It has a clear placement label naming the campaign/use case and platform/format.
- [ ] The audience, message, offer/information and CTA fit the funnel stage.
- [ ] The design follows the official brand book exactly and is recognizably distinct from the other five brands.
- [ ] Every graphic element and image contributes to the message or a real brand application.
- [ ] No visible filler, generic placeholder wording, lorem ipsum, or empty labels remain.
- [ ] Working sample names, ingredients, prices or other details are plausible, editable, and flagged in production notes for verification; unsupported claims are omitted.
- [ ] Text is readable, correctly spelled, and consistent in tone; bilingual copy is reviewed by a fluent reader where required.
- [ ] Logos, labels and important copy are editable Figma elements wherever practical, not baked into AI imagery.
- [ ] Layout, dimensions, crop, safe areas, hierarchy, contrast and export are appropriate for the named use.
- [ ] The Figma section/frame is clearly named and organized; source images are not left dumped on the first page.
- [ ] The handoff/production tracker records the exact Figma page, section, frame/node IDs, status, and any facts requiring verification.

**Completion rule:** An asset is not complete merely because a frame exists or an image has been generated. It is complete only after its purpose, brand fit, content accuracy, editability, platform fit, and QA checklist have been checked.
