### Campaign strategy — revised 2026-11-19

- **Core approach:** Six visibly different brands, not six variations of one premium editorial template. Each brand must have its own color behavior, typography, graphic language, image treatment, composition logic, and platform applications.
- **Content architecture:** Every one of the 60 deliverables must have a named funnel role: **TOFU** (awareness/discovery), **MOFU** (education/evaluation), or **BOFU** (conversion/retention). Operational and collateral assets must also state the funnel moment they support.
- **Use-case labels:** Every Figma section and every handoff row must use a plain-language production label such as `TOFU — Brand discovery — Instagram feed poster`, `MOFU — Menu education — 5-slide carousel`, `BOFU — Lunch offer — paid social ad`, `BOFU — In-store conversion — Sintra-board counter sign`, or `MOFU — Product consideration — online-store listing image set`.
- **Channel variety:** Do not make every deliverable an Instagram post. Use feed posts, carousels, stories, paid ads, Reels, product-page images, marketplace listing covers, print menus, table inserts, counter signs, packaging, box sleeves, product labels, and other genuinely useful touchpoints where relevant.
- **No filler posts:** Every asset must answer: who is it for, what funnel stage does it serve, where will it be used, what should the viewer/customer do next, and why does this brand need it?
- **Brand differentiation:** Use the revised design systems below as hard visual boundaries. Do not default to serif headlines, beige/cream backgrounds, muted terracotta, luxury still-life photography, or the same grid across all brands.
- **Approval checkpoint:** The original preproduction direction is superseded by this revised handoff. Do not begin Figma redesign until the user explicitly greenlights it. Once greenlit, proceed through the approved revised matrix without requesting approval after every asset.

### Global creative and production requirements — revised

- **Funnel label is mandatory:** Each deliverable must carry its funnel stage, use case, format, placement, and intended action in its name and production note.
- **Purpose before format:** A post/carousel/story/ad/Reel is not a strategy by itself. The matrix in Section 3 is the new source of truth for the purpose and placement of each item.
- **Concrete working content:** Do not typeset generic bracket text such as `[product name]`, `[ingredient]`, `[price]`, `[benefit]`, or `[CTA]` in the revised concepts. Use the concrete sample product names, ingredients, menu items, prices, and copy proposed in this handoff so the design can look finished. These are deliberate creative assumptions supplied for prototyping and can be changed during post-production.
- **Truth and verification:** Sample product formulations, ingredient percentages, menu descriptions, product specifications, prices, bundle contents, promotions, stock, and availability are not confirmed business facts. Mark them as *working creative assumptions* in handoff notes; verify or replace them before public release. Never invent testimonials, customer identities, certifications, medical results, scarcity, awards, origin claims, delivery terms, or QR destinations.
- **Customer content:** Never fabricate reviews or UGC. Where real customer material is unavailable, create a community invitation, styling prompt, or clearly empty capture slot—not a fake testimonial.
- **Image generation:** The user previously authorized Figma Generate Image for now. Use it for new Figma-bound visual assets when suitable, but keep generated concept products clearly identified in handoff notes and replace them with actual SKUs where product accuracy matters.
- **Brand-specific production:** Use the correct brand page, correct color system, correct type system, and correct asset naming. Do not place one brand's generated assets on another brand's page. Maintain editable text and vector artwork.
- **Existing designs:** Do not edit, delete, rename, or overwrite any existing Figma design during this replan. Keep them as legacy references. After greenlight, create new sections/frames on the correct existing brand page using a revision suffix or clearly named new section; preserve the old work until the revised version is checked.
- **Quality gate:** Check the funnel logic, format fit, copy, real-world placement, brand distinction, readability, safe areas, asset accuracy, and CTA before marking a deliverable complete.


## 3. CONTENT PLAN

### Revised content strategy and naming convention

This revised architecture supersedes the older 60-piece overview and original visual directions below. Each item has a specific funnel stage and destination. A collateral item is still assigned to the funnel stage it supports.

- **TOFU — Top of funnel:** discovery, brand recognition, reach, shareability, first interaction.
- **MOFU — Middle of funnel:** education, comparison, product understanding, menu/category discovery, fit or usage confidence.
- **BOFU — Bottom of funnel:** order, purchase, booking, add-to-cart, store visit, conversion, repeat purchase, or post-purchase retention.
- **Required use label:** `FUNNEL — objective — platform/placement — format`. Example: `BOFU — Weekday lunch conversion — table insert + counter menu board`.
- **Creative data rule:** the proposed names, ingredients, product combinations, sample menu descriptions, sizes, and prices below are deliberate working concepts so the layouts can contain real-looking content instead of generic brackets. They are not verified business facts. Keep them as typeset sample content during the first design pass; replace them during post-production once the brand's actual catalog, formula, menu, or pricing is supplied.
