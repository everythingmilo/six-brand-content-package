### Morrow Bakehouse — contemporary bakery graphics / abstract vector identity

**Category:** Food & Beverage · Artisan bakery & café  
**Positioning:** A little warmth, every day. Modern neighborhood bakery with a graphic-design-led identity: lively, clear, tactile, and contemporary—not rustic, vintage, or luxury patisserie.

**Color system:** 60% Paper Flour `#FAF7F0` · 30% Ink `#242A33` · 10% Electric Cobalt `#3561D9`. Butter `#E8C36B` and Tomato `#E85B45` are controlled supporting accents. Retire the previous brown-and-terracotta-heavy treatment.

**Typography:** Archivo Black (display/headlines) · DM Sans (body/menu/UI) · IBM Plex Mono (prices, item codes, labels). No Fraunces or handwritten script as a dominant identity device.

**Graphic language:** Abstract vector pastries, geometric bread silhouettes, scored-line patterns, crumb/dot systems, oversized type, modular menu grids, flat color fields, bold crop shapes, simple motion loops, and playful ingredient icons. Food photography is used as a product proof point, not as the only design language. Use real food where appetite appeal is essential; use vector art for identity, menu education, and signage.

**Voice:** Warm, direct, contemporary, lightly playful. Short active copy. Keep the neighborhood-bakery warmth, but remove nostalgic flourishes and precious artisan clichés.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be bold vector bakery graphics, modern sans typography, and cobalt/ink/flour contrast—not a serif headline over a beige food still life.
