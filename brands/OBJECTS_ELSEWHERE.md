### Objects & Elsewhere — Korean-inspired bilingual select-store identity

**Category:** Retail · Curated lifestyle & home goods online store  
**Positioning:** Good things find their place. Present the store as a design-conscious Korean-inspired select shop: precise, collectible, organized, and a little playful. Use Korean visual language as part of the brand system, not as an unverified claim that the products are made in Korea.

**Color system:** 60% Cloud White `#F5F5F2` · 30% Carbon `#202326` · 10% Deep Terracotta Red `#963D32`. Mist Grey `#D9DEDC` and muted Blue Grey `#AAB7BC` are supporting tones. Replace the previous bright Persimmon with the deeper terracotta accent.

**Typography:** Noto Sans KR (Hangul headlines and Korean copy) · Inter (English body and product descriptions) · IBM Plex Mono (SKU, collection codes, size and product data). Build a consistent bilingual hierarchy; do not simply insert isolated Hangul characters as decoration.

**Bilingual treatment:** Use English and Korean together in brand introductions, category labels, collection covers, packaging, and select-store signage. Working brand line: `좋은 물건은 제자리를 찾습니다` / `Good things find their place.` Working category line: `일상을 위한 오브제` / `Objects for everyday living.` Have Hangul wording and line breaks checked by a fluent Korean reader before public release.

**Graphic language:** Seoul select-shop cues: precise modular grids, compact bilingual labels, neatly cropped product photography, retail catalog cards, restrained line icons, hang tags, product stickers, numbered series, editorial price cards, and minimal packaging. Use Korean typography as functional communication, not faux calligraphy or random decorative glyphs.

**Voice:** Curious, considered, useful, concise, lightly witty. Never imply Korean manufacturing, licensing, or provenance unless confirmed.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be bilingual Korean/English retail design, catalog-like structure, and deep terracotta—not a generic western minimalist homeware brand.
