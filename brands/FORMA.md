### FORMA — technical street-performance footwear identity

**Category:** Fashion · Contemporary everyday footwear  
**Positioning:** Made for the in-between. A design-led daily footwear identity for movement between plans, places, and routines. The brand should feel like contemporary performance/streetwear, not fashion-editorial luxury.

**Color system:** 60% Chalk `#F4F2ED` · 30% Graphite `#252932` · 10% Signal Lime `#CFFD3A`. Cobalt `#315DEB` and Concrete `#B9BDC2` are supporting accents.

**Typography:** Barlow Condensed (display/headlines) · Inter (body/product information) · Space Mono (labels and verified product data). Use large condensed headlines, clear information hierarchy, and restrained technical labeling.

**Graphic language:** Dynamic diagonal layouts, tread-inspired graphic rhythms, technical callout lines, movement cues, split-use-case layouts, high-contrast colorway cards, size/fit diagrams, and annotated product cutaways. Treat patterns as graphic cues, not evidence of product construction. Show construction details only when supported by the real model and product specification.

**Voice:** Confident, concise, practical, and contemporary. Focus on design, use cases, fit information, and verified construction. Avoid unsupported comfort, durability, or performance claims.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be technical street footwear: condensed typography, dynamic geometry, graphite/chalk contrast, and signal-lime detail.
