### FORMA — technical street-performance footwear identity

**Category:** Fashion · Contemporary everyday footwear  
**Positioning:** Made for the in-between. Design-led daily footwear presented through movement, construction, fit, and city utility. The brand should feel like contemporary performance/streetwear, not fashion-editorial luxury.

**Color system:** 60% Chalk `#F4F2ED` · 30% Graphite `#252932` · 10% Signal Lime `#CFFD3A`. Cobalt `#315DEB` and Concrete `#B9BDC2` are supporting accents.

**Typography:** Barlow Condensed (display/headlines) · Inter (body/product information) · Space Mono (size, model code, construction data). Large condensed type, technical labeling, and strong numeric hierarchy.

**Graphic language:** Dynamic diagonal layouts, outsole-tread patterns, technical callout lines, movement arrows, split-screen use cases, high-contrast colorway cards, size/fit diagrams, annotated product cutaways, street photography, and performance-inspired motion graphics. Only show construction features supported by the real product model/spec sheet.

**Voice:** Confident, concise, practical, and contemporary. Focus on design, use cases, fit information, and verifiable construction. Avoid unsupported comfort, durability, or performance claims.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be technical street footwear: condensed typography, dynamic geometry, graphite/chalk contrast, and signal-lime detail—not an understated premium footwear campaign.
