### AURELLE Skin — vibrant sunset orange / pure-white skincare identity

**Category:** Beauty & Personal Care · Skincare  
**Positioning:** Skin, in its own time. Reinterpret the calm positioning through an energetic, optimistic, contemporary skincare system. Keep the reassurance in the voice, not in a muted palette.

**Color system:** 60% Pure White `#FFFFFF` · 30% Sunset Orange `#FF6A35` · 10% Near Black `#202124`. Soft Apricot `#FFE0D2` and Tangerine `#FF965F` are supporting tints. Remove Porcelain/Oat/Sage/Olive as the dominant system.

**Typography:** Manrope ExtraBold (display/headlines) · Manrope Regular/Medium (body) · IBM Plex Mono (formula names, ingredient data, product codes). No serif display type.

**Graphic language:** Strong orange color-blocking, oversized sans headlines, white-on-orange and orange-on-white layouts, sun-disc/radiating shapes, rounded formula cards, punchy numbered steps, vivid macro texture photography, bright studio product shots, clean iconography, and compact data labels. Maintain large white breathing spaces so the orange remains intentional, not overwhelming.

**Voice:** Informed, friendly, clear, reassuring, and confident. Explain ingredients in plain language. No diagnoses, guaranteed results, fear-based language, or unsupported clinical claims.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be vivid sunset orange, pure white, bold sans typography, and clean skincare/product visuals. Do not carry over the old quiet botanical beige/serif direction.
