### OLFACT — nocturnal fragrance-lab / sensory archive identity

**Category:** Beauty & Retail · Online perfume store  
**Positioning:** A scent of your own. Treat fragrance discovery as a sensory archive: precise, exploratory, atmospheric, and knowledgeable. Avoid another conventional luxury-perfume serif campaign.

**Color system:** 60% Midnight `#15191F` · 30% Vapor Silver `#E7EAEC` · 10% Electric Violet `#8B79FF`. Supporting tones: Smoke `#777E87` and translucent Lavender `#C8C0FF`.

**Typography:** Space Grotesk (display) · Manrope (product/e-commerce copy) · IBM Plex Mono (scent codes, note pyramids, concentration, volume, and product data). No Bodoni or Cormorant as the default.

**Graphic language:** Dark technical layouts, scent-note maps, ingredient/specimen cards, molecular/airflow-inspired line diagrams (illustrative, not scientific claims), ultraviolet light, reflective glass, metallic foil accents, barcode-like product codes, scent family matrices, and concise note pyramids. Use macro ingredient imagery with clear labels, not vague atmospheric fog alone.

**Voice:** Sensory, evocative, specific, and knowledgeable. Explain how notes smell and combine in plain language. Avoid unsupported longevity, projection, ingredient safety, or authenticity claims.

**Distinctness rule:** The first impression should be a dark fragrance archive/lab with ultraviolet accent and technical note graphics—not warm beige luxury perfume editorial.
